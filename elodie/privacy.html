Privacy Policy - Elodie Poster

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Elodie Poster is a private app used only to publish content to, and reply to comments on, the Instagram account @elodie.ashworth, an openly AI-generated virtual creator. It is operated by the account owner and is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

  • Access to the @elodie.ashworth Instagram professional account, granted by its owner, to publish posts and reels and to read and reply to comments on its own posts.
  • The text of public comments left on @elodie.ashworth's posts, and the commenter's public username, only to write a reply to that comment.
  • Basic statistics for the account's own posts (such as views, likes, saves and shares), only to decide what to post next.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, share or rent any data to anyone.
  • It does not build profiles of people, send direct messages, or follow, like or contact other accounts.
  • It does not access any account other than @elodie.ashworth.

Storage

Comment text is processed only at the moment a reply is written and is not stored long-term. A short record of which comments have already been answered (comment IDs only) is kept so the same comment is not answered twice.

Deleting your data

See our data deletion instructions, or email elodieashworth.official@gmail.com.
