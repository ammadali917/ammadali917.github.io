Data Deletion Instructions - Elodie Poster

Elodie Poster only works with the Instagram account @elodie.ashworth and does not keep personal data about other people. The only thing it remembers is the ID of comments it has already replied to.

If you commented on @elodie.ashworth and want any record of your comment removed:

  1. Email elodieashworth.official@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your Instagram username.
  2. We will delete any record linked to your comments within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also delete your own comment on Instagram at any time, and remove any app's access to your account in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.
